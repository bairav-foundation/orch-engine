# Code of conduct

Be kind, be patient, assume good intent. People here come from different countries, languages and levels of experience; questions are welcome at every level.

Not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, insults about who someone is, sharing someone's private information, or sexual content. Disagree with ideas, not people.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, email hello@bairavs.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or block accounts that break these rules.

This is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
